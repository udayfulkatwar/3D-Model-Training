## What we sell (current)

2D floor plans turned into browser-based interactive 3D, plus walkthrough videos
and stills: first-person walkthrough, material/finish customization,
morning-to-night lighting, shareable link, no install.

**Differentiator:** an interactive experience on a phone from one link, at a
price far below premium international studios. Local competition is mostly
offline 3ds Max + V-Ray video, which is not interactive.

## Client-communication rules

- Never say: "100% accurate", "exact sunlight study", "works on every phone",
  "AI makes it automatically", "unlimited changes", or any invented project count.
- Say: "follows your drawings closely", "lighting is illustrative",
  "tested on target devices", "built from your drawings by our team with
  AI-assisted tools".
- Never use another company's name, logo, images or designs in our material.
